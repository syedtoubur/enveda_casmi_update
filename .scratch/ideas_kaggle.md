## 2. Top Orthogonal Ideas (Not in `casmi26-e006`)

### 🌟 Idea 1: FragGap — Untrained MetFrag-Lite Fragment Evidence Fallback
- **Source Notebooks**:
  - `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-v7c-e006-fraggap` (Ahmed Berat Özer, **Public LB 0.441**, 62 upvotes)
  - `nursrijan/casm26-e006-with-metfrag-lite-re-rank` (Nur Srijan, LB 0.441)
- **The Gap in Baseline E006**:
  In E006, molecules without `[M+H]+` spectra get skipped by GLACIER. If ICEBERG also fails or provides no finite scores, the molecule falls into an **evidence gap**—its candidates retain raw engine-1 / prefusion order with zero fragmentation evidence.
- **The Mechanism**:
  `v7c` embeds an in-memory, zero-dependency, Numba-accelerated **MetFrag-lite fragmentation explainer** (`frag_rescore.py`):
  1. For every candidate SMILES, it enumerates neutral fragment masses generated by breaking $\le 2$ bonds of the heavy-atom graph.
  2. Matches candidate fragment masses against experimental MS/MS peaks under allowable adduct carriers and hydrogen shifts ($\Delta H \in [-2, +3]$, tolerance $0.005\text{ Da}$).
  3. Computes the intensity-weighted explained fragment ratio (assigning weight 1.0 for 1-bond cleavages, 0.6 for 2-bond cleavages).
  4. Only applies to molecules where GLACIER and ICEBERG provided **zero coverage** (`not gl and not ice_covered`), re-ranking candidates with weight $\lambda = 0.50$.
- **Leaderboard Impact**: Lifted E006 from **0.440 to 0.441** on Public LB.
- **Repo Suitability**: Direct graft into `build/casmi_post.py` as an additional fallback branch before defaulting to prefusion.

### 🌟 Idea 2: Multi-Collision-Energy Spectrum Merging for GLACIER (`_gl_mce_pkg`)
- **Source Notebook**: `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-v7e-e006-glmultice`
- **The Gap in Baseline E006**:
  In `test.parquet`, roughly 25% of spectra are **summed / multi-collision-energy** acquisitions (e.g., collision energies `[20, 40, 60]` or `[20, 50]` eV). Baseline GLACIER evaluates only a single scalar collision energy bucket (`ce_bucket`), losing valuable fragmentation information or generating noisy predictions for composite spectra.
- **The Mechanism**:
  `v7e` dynamically patches `gl_runner.py` in-memory:
  1. Identifies merged spectra with multiple collision energy entries.
  2. Evaluates the candidate forward simulation across each listed collision energy bucket.
  3. Computes spectral entropy similarity for each CE condition and aggregates via `meansim` or `maxsim`:
     $$\text{Score}(c) = \frac{1}{|CE|} \sum_{ce \in CE} \text{EntropySim}(S_{\text{query}}, S_{\text{pred}}(c, ce))$$
- **Leaderboard Impact**: Refines simulator discrimination on merged spectra without changing candidate sets.
- **Repo Suitability**: Exploit line patch for the GLACIER invocation script.

### 🌟 Idea 3: In-Silico Proton-Frame Normalization for `[M+Na]+` GLACIER Coverage
- **Source Notebook**: `drpatrickchan/casmi26-v7d-e007-na-glacier` (Patrick Chan)
- **The Gap in Baseline E006**:
  GLACIER was trained strictly on protonated adducts `[M+H]+`. Any evaluation query whose only high-quality spectra are sodiated `[M+Na]+` receives zero GLACIER cross-attention and drops out to fallback.
- **The Mechanism**:
  `v7d` introduces **in-silico adduct normalization**:
  1. For queries where `[M+Na]+` is the only available spectrum, it shifts every peak mass by:
     $$\Delta m = m(\text{H}^+) - m(\text{Na}^+) = 1.007825 - 22.989769 = -21.98194\text{ Da}$$
  2. Relabels the query spectrum as `[M+H]+` and runs GLACIER on the shifted spectrum.
  3. While in-source sodiated fragments differ in reality, major neutral-loss and backbone fragments preserve delta-mass connectivity, granting GLACIER meaningful discrimination on otherwise blind molecules.
- **Repo Suitability**: High potential for `research/explore/` as an adduct-coverage booster.

### 🌟 Idea 4: DreaMS Deep Neural Spectrum Embedding Retrieval & Tail Backfill
- **Source Notebooks**:
  - `hengck23/dreams-structure-from-spectrum-retrival-demo` (Heng CherKeng)
  - `shafiquatali/casmi26-pub-dreams`
  - `obstacledeveloper/casmi26-v1-1-metric-audit-and-safe-tail`
- **The Gap in Baseline E006**:
  E006 relies exclusively on 6,930-bit FPNet dot-products for structural candidate retrieval. For molecules where FPNet fails to retrieve the correct structure into the top 40/60 pool, the ceiling is zero. Furthermore, many test queries have fewer than 25 candidates after deduplication.
- **The Mechanism**:
  1. **Neural Spectrum Embeddings**: Uses Bushuiev et al.'s pretrained DreaMS transformer (`hengck23-dreams-enveda-casmi26`) to encode raw MS/MS spectra directly into deep embedding vectors.
  2. **Embedding KNN Retrieval**: Computes cosine KNN against pre-embedded reference libraries (`MassSpecGym_DreaMS_embeddings.npy` or `Enveda180_DreaMS_index.parquet`).
  3. **Metric Tail Backfill**: In `casmi26-v1-1-metric-audit-and-safe-tail`, DreaMS candidates within $\pm 10\text{ ppm}$ precursor mass are appended into vacant slots (slots 15–25) after official tautomer deduplication, guaranteeing a full 25-candidate submission without filler noise.
- **External Dataset**: `hengck23-dreams-enveda-casmi26`
- **Repo Suitability**: Candidate pool expansion for Class 2/3 and tail backfill.

### 🌟 Idea 5: Fusion-First Simulator Coverage (Evidence Gap Healing)
- **Source Notebook**: `flexonafft/casmi26-closing-the-evidence-gap` (Igor Zharov)
- **The Gap in Baseline E006**:
  When candidates are merged into the top-40 shortlist, physics simulators (ICEBERG/GLACIER) sometimes finish with missing scores for only 1 or 2 candidates in a same-formula cluster. When a simulator has partial coverage in a cluster, baseline code either discards the whole group or leaves missing candidates arbitrarily ranked.
- **The Mechanism**:
  Runs a targeted secondary simulator pass exclusively on unfinished candidates within same-formula comparison groups. A same-formula cluster is re-ranked only when **100% complete finite coverage** is achieved across all competing isomers, preventing arbitrary order retention.
- **Repo Suitability**: Post-processing refinement in `build/casmi_post.py`.

## 4. Documented Pitfalls, Traps, and Failed Hypotheses

Forensic analyses by Maman Salto (`casmi26-forensic-lineage-and-traps-analysis`), Kratosyan (`casmi-2026-five-data-facts-that-break-validation`), and Varshitha Kolupuri (`casmi26-real-scores-and-what-top-notebooks-use`) reveal critical traps that actively drop LB score:

### ⚠️ Trap 1: The External Library Rank-1 Promotion Trap (Drop: -0.009 LB)
- **The Temptation**: Finding an exact cosine match ($\ge 0.50$) in MassBank or external GNPS libraries and forcing it into Rank 1.
- **Why It Fails**: On the blind test set (~130 evaluation molecules), authentic external standards of closely related isomers or chemical analogs frequently match peaks with high cosine. Forcing these into Rank 1 displaces the true ground truth already placed by the calibrated FPNet + GLACIER/ICEBERG pipeline. `saddamaditya` verified this dropped LB from **0.439 to 0.430**.

### ⚠️ Trap 2: The "Sovereign Library Hard Shield" Fallacy (Drop: -0.004 LB)
- **The Temptation**: Whenever `lib_max >= 0.90` against the training library, locking the candidate in Rank 1.
- **Why It Fails**: On training data, this looks optimal (+0.26 offline MRR). But on the blind test set (Class 2/3), a 0.90 match in train is almost always a structural neighbor/isomer rather than the exact molecule. Forward simulators (GLACIER/ICEBERG) frequently find the correct isomer, but the hard shield overrides it with the decoy neighbor.

### ⚠️ Trap 3: Visible Test Set Leakage (False 1.0 Offline MRR)
- **The Fact**: 100% of visible `test.parquet` spectra (1,213 of 1,213) exist inside `train.parquet` from `enveda-180` with cosine $\ge 0.999$. Any nearest-neighbor lookup on visible test achieves ~1.000 MRR, which is completely non-predictive of the hidden competition test set. Validation MUST strictly hold out entire structures by `InChIKey14`.

### ⚠️ Trap 4: Raw InChIKey vs Tautomer-Canonical InChIKey14
- **The Fact**: **10.43% of competition structures** have a tautomer-canonical `InChIKey14` that differs from their raw InChIKey. Candidate deduplication must use `TautomerEnumerator().Canonicalize()` with RDKit 2026.03.3; otherwise, tautomeric duplicate slots are submitted, wasting valuable candidate spots in the top 25.
