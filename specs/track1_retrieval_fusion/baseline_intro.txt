1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & COMPETITION OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Competition: Enveda CASMI 2026 - Molecule ID From Mass Spectra.
- Core Objective: Predict 2D molecular structures (as SMILES) directly from tandem
  mass spectrometry data (LC-MS/MS: precursor m/z, adduct, collision energy, 
  and fragment peak m/z and intensities).
- Official Metric: Mean Reciprocal Rank at 25 (MRR@25).
    * Rank 1  -> 1.000
    * Rank 2  -> 0.500
    * Rank 25 -> 0.040
    * Rank > 25 or absent -> 0.000
- Scoring Equivalence:
    * Predictions and answers are stripped of stereochemistry and tautomer forms.
    * Evaluated strictly via RDKit tautomer canonicalization (pinned to 2026.03.3) 
      reduced to the first 14 characters of the InChIKey (InChIKey14).
    * Submitting duplicates or stereoisomers of the same connectivity wastes 
      candidate slots without extra credit.
- Submission Constraints:
    * Kaggle Notebook submission (Internet disabled, offline only).
    * Runtime <= 9 hours (32,400s) on single GPU (Nvidia T4/P100).
    * Output format: `submission.csv` with exactly 400 test molecules, up to 25 
      semicolon-separated candidate SMILES per row.


2. THE SOTA BASELINE (v4n - 0.384 LB) END-TO-END PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The current state-of-the-art solution (`casmi26-v4n-inference (1).ipynb`) is a 
7-cell modular engine structured into 5 sequential operational stages:

Stage 1: Precursor Mass & Neutral Mass Estimation
- Extracts precursor m/z and adduct string (e.g., [M+H]+, [M-H]-, [M+Na]+, [2M+H]+).
- Computes exact neutral monoisotopic mass via calibrated adduct equation offsets.
- Narrow mass search window: +/- 10 ppm (fallback to 30 ppm only if 0 candidates found).

Stage 2: External Giant-Scale PubChem Search Channel (Isolated Subprocess)
- Why: The training set and natural product databases (COCONUT) do not contain 
  every molecule. PubChem contains >100M structures.
- How: Runs as an isolated background subprocess (`pc_runner.py`) using memory-mapped
  NumPy arrays (>6 GB) to avoid RAM/GPU memory collisions.
- Step 1: Precursor mass filtering narrows millions of PubChem structures to a few
  thousand candidates within +/- 10 ppm.
- Step 2 (Pass 1 Coarse Screen): Rapid matrix multiplication against standard 4,096-bit 
  ECFP4 fingerprints to keep the top PC_N1 = 5,000 candidates (expanded from 1,000 in v4h).
- Step 3 (Pass 2 Fine Scoring): Computes full 10,226-bit fingerprints and evaluates the 
  exact Bayes log-likelihood dot product:
      Score = f . z
  where f is the candidate's actual fingerprint and z is the FPNet consensus logit vector.
- Step 4 (Deduplication): Sorts descending, converts to InChIKey14, discards structures 
  already present in the Main Pool (Train + COCONUT), and outputs top 25 novel PubChem structures.

Stage 3: High-Speed Reference Cache Construction
- Reads `train.parquet` (2,539,608 spectra, 275,810 structures, 133,228,108 peaks).
- Builds memory-mapped index in `/kaggle/working/work/` for sub-millisecond spectral cosine matching.

Stage 4: Main Pool Candidate Retrieval & Analogue Generation
- Candidate Universe: Main Pool of 710,701 structures (274,186 from Training set + 
  436,515 from COCONUT 2.0 natural products).
- On-The-Fly Analogue Derivations: When exact library matches are absent, the engine 
  derives modified candidate graphs from spectral analogues to match the exact precursor mass.

Stage 5: 160-Feature Extraction & LightGBM Ranking
- Extracts 160 engineered tabular features across 5 families (`fe_v4`):
    1. `derivation`: Structure generation priors & Class-3 generation flags.
    2. `frag2`: Fragmentation 2.0 (subformula matching, peak explainability, neutral losses).
    3. `analog_struct`: Scaffold relations to spectral analogues in the training library.
    4. `model_views`: Consensus across single vs merged spectra and polarity views.
    5. `fragnet`: Learned fragment scores and training library cosine similarity (`lib_max`).
- Scored by an ensemble of 4 LightGBM Boosters:
      rank_score = mean(Booster_1..4(160 features))
- Retains top 60 best candidates (`TOPN = 60`).

Stage 6: Forward Physics Spectrum Simulation (Isomer Disambiguation)
- Problem: Constitutional isomers sharing the exact same formula have identical mass 
  and nearly identical 2D fingerprints; tree models struggle to distinguish them.
- Solution: Two physical forward spectrum simulators predict expected MS/MS peaks:
    1. ICEBERG: GNN-based fragmentation model (from `ms-pred`, budget 2,700s).
    2. GLACIER: 15.1M-parameter Transformer model trained on MassSpecGym (budget 2,400s).
- Equal-Weight Rescoring Formula (v4n update): Within each molecular formula group:
      Score_formula = z(LightGBM) + 1.0 * z(ICEBERG) + 1.0 * z(GLACIER)
  where z() is within-formula z-score normalization.

Stage 7: Confident Decision Gate & Interleaved Submission Merge
- Compares Main Pool candidates with PubChem candidates using two key signals:
    1. `lib_max`: Peak cosine match against the measured training library.
    2. `Delta`: Score gap between PubChem top FPNet score and Main Pool top FPNet score.
- Decision Rules:
    * If `lib_max >= 0.90`: Strong library hit -> Keep Main Pool list exclusively.
    * If `lib_max < 0.90` and `Delta > 600`: Aggressive PubChem insertion at ranks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.
    * If `lib_max < 0.90` and `Delta <= 600`: Gentle PubChem insertion at ranks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20.
- Outputs top 25 deduplicated InChIKey14 SMILES into `submission.csv`.


3. COMPLETE INVENTORY OF MODELS & ARTIFACTS IN v4n
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FPNet Models (Neural Fingerprint Transformers):
   - Main Engine Backbone: `fpnet_full1.pt` (Dataset: `casmi26-fpnet-full1`).
     Predicts 10,226 fingerprint bits from MS/MS spectra and metadata.
   - PubChem Screening Ensemble: `fpnet_0.pt` and `fpnet_1.pt` (Dataset: `casmi26-v3-models`).
     Averaged in `ModelBank` to rank PubChem structures via dot product.

2. Primary Ranker:
   - Framework: LightGBM (`lightgbm.Booster`).
   - Architecture: 4 Boosters averaged together.
   - Package: `casmi26-v4b-models` (loaded via manifest).
   - Input: 160 engineered features from `fe_v4`.

3. Forward Isomer Simulators:
   - ICEBERG: GNN forward fragmentation predictor (`casmi26-iceberg`, weight = 1.0).
   - GLACIER: Transformer forward spectrum predictor (`casmi26-glacier`, weight = 1.0, 
     15,108,154 parameters).

4. Reference Datasets & Caches:
   - Main Pool: `pool_meta.parquet`, `pool_fp.npy`, `pool_frag_*.npy` (`casmi26-v2-pool`, 710k).
   - PubChem Tier: `pc_mass.npy`, `pc_off.npy`, `pc_smiles.npy` (`casmi26-pubchem-tier`, >6 GB).
   - Training Library: Competition `train.parquet` (2.54M spectra).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      z(LightGBM) + 0.5 * z(ICEBERG) + 0.5 * z(GLACIER)

- v4l (LB 0.380): Doubled forward physical simulation weights:
      ICE_LAM = GL_LAM = 1.0 (gave forward physics equal voice with LightGBM).

- v4n (LB 0.384): Unified combination of v4i + v4l + v4m:
      5,000 PubChem depth + lambda=1.0 forward simulators + fpnet_full1 backbone.
      Runtime: ~5,538 seconds (~92 min) on single GPU.


5. DIAGNOSTIC LEVERS FROM ALTERNATIVE ARCHITECTURES (TIER-1 PATCHED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Analysis of `enveda-casmi-2026-tier1-patched.ipynb` revealed four high-value 
empirical physics/data corrections that have NOT yet been integrated into v4n:

1. Systematic Precursor Offset Re-Centering (+1.45 ppm):
   - Finding: timsTOF instruments run systematically +1.45 ppm high relative to exact 
     formula mass.
   - Tier-1 fix: Centered the query mass `target / (1 + 1.45e-6)`.
   - Benefit: Allows shrinking search window from 10 ppm to 6–8.5 ppm with 95%+ recall, 
     drastically shedding decoy false positives.

2. Exact Structural Mass Indexing (`STRUCT_MASS = True`):
   - Finding: Upstream reference sources (e.g. RIKEN, 341k spectra) truncated precursor m/z 
     to 2 decimal places, causing 30% of their spectra to fall outside +/- 8.5 ppm.
   - Tier-1 fix: Indexed the reference cache on the label's exact formula mass.
   - Benefit: +0.006 MRR boost; recovered 2,401 previously unreachable natural products.

3. Peak Filtering Optimization (`MAX_PEAKS = 64` vs 256):
   - Finding: Truncating MS2 peaks to the top 64 cleanest peaks improved spectral entropy 
     similarity by +0.003 MRR by cutting instrument noise floor.

4. Mass-Shifted Analogue Propagation:
   - Implemented wide (+/- 200 Da) mass-shifted spectral entropy matching ($sim^4 \times \text{Tanimoto}$).
   - Could be engineered into v4n's feature matrix.


6. ARCHITECTURAL BOTTLENECKS & STRATEGIC DIRECTIONS TO EXCEED 0.384
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To push beyond 0.384, improvements must target the 3 official novelty classes:

Class 1: Known in Training Library
- Status: Solved (>90% accuracy). Protected by `lib_max >= 0.90`.
- Opportunity: RIKEN exact mass indexing and +1.45 ppm re-centering can squeeze extra 
  recall out of borderline library matches.

Class 2: Known in External Databases (PubChem/COCONUT), Unmeasured
- Status: Partially solved (MRR ~0.50-0.65). Powered by FPNet and PubChem channel.
- Opportunities:
  * Integrate additional natural product databases into the fast pool (ChEBI, NPAtlas, 
    LIPID MAPS) so they don't have to compete for the 25 PubChem slots.
  * Deepen PubChem Pass 2 or refine Pass 1 filtering.
  * Improve FPNet ensembling (combining multiple diverse FPNet checkpoints).

Class 3: Truly Novel Natural Products (Absent from PubChem and Training)
- Status: Major current bottleneck (MRR < 0.20).
- Opportunities:
  * De novo structure generation: Incorporating diffusion / generative models 
    (e.g., MADGEN, MSNovelist, or expanded derivation engines).
  * In-silico scaffold propagation: Propagating Murcko scaffolds from mass-shifted 
    spectral analogues (as described in MADGEN / MassSpecGym).
  * Enhancing forward simulation: Negative-mode forward simulation (currently ICEBERG 
    only runs on positive mode) and tuning forward simulation weights or blending 
    DreaMS spectral embeddings.
