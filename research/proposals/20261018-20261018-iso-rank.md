## P2: a pre-registered gate on overall pseudo_c3 delta let a tail ride-along stand in for the mechanism

### Text
When an idea combines a triggered action with an unconditional ride-along (here an insert plus a rank 21-25 tail fill), pre-register the fire-only delta and the ride-along-only delta as separate gate numbers, with the bar on the triggered part. An overall delta can clear a bar from the ride-along alone, and the ride-along ceiling (about +0.001 for tail-only) is already known. Also report the selection-half delta next to the verdict half: a drop from half A to half B is selection optimism, not a wobble.

### Reasoning
Stage A's verdict was DEAD against the 0.012 bar, but only the extra fire-only number showed why: the +0.0015 was the tail (fire-only -0.0003), matching the charter's tail-only ceiling of about +0.001. Half A had shown +0.0050 and half B +0.0015, a 3x shrink after list and m_ice selection. Had the bar been lower, the overall number would have passed on a mechanism the spec did not claim.
