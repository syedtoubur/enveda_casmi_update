## 1. JESTR: Joint Embedding Space Technique for Ranking molecules from spectra
- Link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12233093/ · Year: 2025 (24 cites)
- Method: contrastive (CLIP-style) training of a spectrum encoder and a molecule encoder
  into one joint space; rank candidates by embedding **cosine similarity** to the query
  spectrum, not by predict-then-match.
- `plugs in at:` rank 2–5 ordering (C2) and C1 cross-instrument order — a *direct*
  spectrum↔candidate similarity is an evidence type the pipeline lacks (it uses FPNet
  `f·z` and forward-sim entropy only).
- Licence/weights/training: open-access paper; code/weights status to VERIFY (check repo).
  Training data = public MS/MS (assumed MassSpecGym/GNPS class → MCES leak audit needed).
- Depth: abstract + method. Confidence: med-high on mechanism, low on offline-weights.

## 2. MIST + MIST-CF (Metabolite Inference with Spectrum Transformers)
- Link: https://github.com/samgoldman97/mist · MIST-CF https://github.com/samgoldman97/mist-cf · Year: 2023 (56 cites)
- Method: transformer maps MS/MS peaks → molecular **fingerprints** (predict-then-match)
  and, in a contrastive variant, → a joint spectrum↔molecule space for **direct retrieval**;
  MIST-CF annotates the precursor **chemical formula** de novo.
- `plugs in at:` (a) contrastive retrieval = rank 2–5 ordering; (b) MIST-CF formula
  posterior = an inter-formula prior for pool-miss/formula ordering.
- Licence/weights/training: GitHub, permissive (verify exact licence; reusability report
  #11 says a retrained MIST ≈ public weights). Trained on NPLIB1/MassSpecGym-class data.
- Depth: skim (repo + abstract). Confidence: high mechanism, med weights-availability.

## 3. DreaMS — self-supervised tandem-MS foundation model
- Link: https://github.com/pluskal-lab/DreaMS · Nature Biotech 2025/2026 (137 cites) · https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02663-3
- Method: transformer pre-trained self-supervised on 24M unannotated spectra (GNMS);
  emits per-token / whole-spectrum **embeddings** transferable to downstream tasks.
- `plugs in at:` already **vendored and runs on Kaggle T4** (Track 2); embeddings feed a
  cross-encoder / tail-backfill / contrastive head rather than a new model to stage.
- Licence/weights/training: open weights (MIT-class), embeddings released; pretraining on
  unlabelled public spectra.
- Depth: skim. Confidence: high (already in repo, byte-identical G0 PASS).

## 4. GLMR: Generative Modeling for Accurate Molecule Retrieval from MS/MS
- Link: https://arxiv.org/html/2511.06259v1 · AAAI 2026 (7 cites)
- Method: two-stage — a generative retrieval stage proposes candidate molecules, then a
  **cross-encoder** jointly encodes (spectrum, candidate) to refine the ranking; beats
  baselines on MassSpecGym and their MassRET-20k.
- `plugs in at:` rank 2–5 ordering over the fused union (same slot as `v4b-union-xenc`);
  the joint (spectrum,candidate) cross-attention is a genuinely new scorer, not a reweight.
- Licence/weights/training: to VERIFY (AAAI, likely code release pending); MassSpecGym+
  training → leak audit.
- Depth: abstract + method. Confidence: med-high mechanism, low weights-readiness.

## 5. MassSpecGym v1.5 — "Uncovering and Correcting Evaluation Pitfalls"
- Link: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19624v1 · Year: 2026-06
- Method: taxonomy of evaluation failure modes; shows leakage is a *gradient* (structure
  splits, shared components like formula predictors / spectral simulators, synthetic data
  from simulators); recommends MCES≥10 splits and auditing shared pretrained components.
- `plugs in at:` the **instrument** (§A5). Directly confirms why C2-ordering ideas are
  uncitable offline (BASE leak + simulator leak) and names the fix (held-out fold,
  structure-distance split) for an honest cohort.
- Licence/weights/training: benchmark paper; MassSpecGym v1.5 splits released.
- Depth: read (leakage sections). Confidence: high (corroborates measured LESSONS L07/L19).
