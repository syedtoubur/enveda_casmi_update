## Interpretation (honest)

- Sanity: V22 MRR reproduces pseudo_c3 0.0562, C2 0.5907, C1_npx 0.9285, C2_hard 0.5707, C1_xlib 0.9153 (C2_np 0.5432).
- Pre-registered variant (x removed from the rest, then inserted at s): no slot has a positive expected Delta at pi 0.25
  in the other-variant. The argmax is s* = 7 at -0.0009 (90% CI [-0.0027, +0.0007]), i.e. the best fixed slot is
  "do nothing within noise". The all-molecule variant is also <= +0.0003 for every slot at pi 0.25 (slot 4: +0.0003).
- Why pseudo_c3 turns negative from s = 8 on: the literal rule demotes a generated top-1 that V22 already ranked
  above s (leak-hit molecules, Delta -0.16 at s = 25). On "other" pseudo_c3 molecules the gain is monotone
  (+0.044 at s = 2 falling to +0.003 at s = 25).
- Supplementary keep-higher variant (a deployable rule would not demote): expected Delta at pi 0.25 (other) peaks at
  s = 7 with +0.0018, at pi 0.35 +0.0031, at pi 0.15 +0.0006. Small, below the 0.004 LB noise floor, but positive.
  Slot 2 is the worst slot on C1/C2 (about -0.03 to -0.05 per leak-free molecule) and is net negative for every pi in
  both variants despite the largest pseudo_c3 gain (+0.039).
- The C1/C2 cost is the placeholder bound (attack A1): a real insert can only help there.
- timsTOF sub-curve (n = 15) is flag-only: positive only at s = 2, CI spans zero; not distinguishable from noise.
- CIs on the expected curve combine independent cohort bootstraps (scaffold groups, seed 0, 2000); they ignore the
  uncertainty of pi itself.
